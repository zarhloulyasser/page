Questions

Open questions I'd like better answers to. Some are researchable, some probably aren't. If you have a good answer to any of them, I'd like to hear it.

  • What makes one frame better than another, given that no frame is complete? Is there anything to say beyond "it depends what you're doing"?
  • Why do some metaphors become invisible infrastructure — load-bearing for an entire field — while equally apt ones never take?
  • How much of what we call taste is a compressed model of second- and third-order effects that the person can't articulate?
  • Do latent spaces recover something real about the structure of meaning, or mostly the structure of the corpus?
  • Why is experiential design so reliably underrated by the people who most depend on it?
  • What is the smallest change to a system's scoring rule that flips its long-run behaviour — and is that quantity predictable in advance?
  • Can first-principles thinking be taught, or can you only build conditions where refusing to use it becomes uncomfortable?
  • What does it mean for a thing to have an essence, in a way that isn't secretly just a claim about our categories?

Placeholder questions — add and edit freely.
