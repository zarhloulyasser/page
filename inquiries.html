Inquiries

Not prompts. A prompt makes me think: question → verbal answer. I want: question → altered attention. Call them attentional cues or inquiries. No verbal answer required.

One ordinary sacred act

  • What would this be like if I stopped treating it as ordinary? Don't answer. Look.
  • What am I failing to notice because this is familiar?
  • For a person: what changes if I remember that this person is alive, conscious, and temporary?
  • For an activity: what is actually here before I call it ordinary?
  • For sacredness: is there a way of looking at this that makes its sacred dimension visible?
Yasser Zarhloul
