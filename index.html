Hi — I'm Yasser. This is where I keep the things I've thought about long enough to want to write down. Most of it is unfinished on purpose; I'd rather publish a frame I'm still testing than a conclusion I've stopped questioning.

The thread running through all of it is framing. A frame isn't a description of the world — it's a piece of equipment for acting in it. Swap one out and you change what counts as evidence, what registers as a problem, and what feels like an obvious next move. Most disagreements I find interesting turn out to be frame disagreements wearing a costume.

What that pulls in, roughly: metaphor as infrastructure rather than decoration; systems, and the second- and third-order effects that make them surprising; economic thinking as a general-purpose lens rather than a subject; game theory for the places where incentives outrun intentions; latent spaces, and what it means that meaning turns out to have a geometry; experiential design — how an environment teaches you what to want before you've consciously decided anything; and AI, mostly as the strangest ongoing experiment in representation we've run.

Underneath it is one question I keep circling: what is a thing actually like, once you've stripped away the frame you happened to arrive with?

Interested in

Frames, framing, and representations. Metaphors. Economy and economic thinking. Systems and systems thinking. First-principles, second-order and third-order thinking. Experience and experiential design. Game theory. Latent spaces. AI. Writing. The essence of things.
